$1999. It folds. Congratulations humans — it's been a whole month since the iPhone 18 Pro Max, and your favourite S&P 100 company hasn't seen your wallet open since. Nobody asked for the iPhone Duo, so Apple made a [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FUfGcZ092b0) good enough that you'll believe you did.

Slop is now everywhere. Organisations like Apple delivering products as sloppy as the Duo is the reality that every consumer needs to face. They aren't designing products that actually improve life, only to extract the bottom dollar from the middle class through global marketing and branding.

It's almost inconceivable that Apple's iPhone Duo was actually released. It's getting to the point where Apple can offer a pile of dog shit and consumers will rush to pull out their wallets and purchase for the low low price of [$1999 USD](https://www.apple.com/us/shop/buy-iphone/iphone-duo).

Who even wants this garbage. Do you really need a screen that can fold and fit in your pocket? I guess you can goon out on TikTok on a bigger screen now.

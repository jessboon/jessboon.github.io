Technical lead on a platform for pharmaceutical report analysis using [public FDA datasets](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfmaude/search.cfm).

**Role:** Tech Lead · 2024 H2

**Stack:** Python, AWS ECS, AWS RDS PostgreSQL, Redis, Celery, OpenAI, React, Node.js, TypeScript

**Goal:** Build a platform for onboarded pharmaceutical manufacturers to deeply analyse problem and patient reports across a wide range of scopes.

<p><strong>Impact:</strong> <span class="impact">Reduced the time to analyse and understand product issues outside of cross-manufacturer problems by 23%.</span></p>

## Responsibilities

1. Lead a team of 3 engineers across full stack, data, design, and architecture.
2. Liaise with business and leadership on direction, design, and approach.
3. Design and architect:
   1. Data architecture to store FDA dataset data — managing extremely large 50 GB+ .txt-based datasets.
   2. Pipelines to perform transformation, cleansing, aggregation, and embeddings of FDA text reports into consumable data structures.
   3. Async workflow architecture to handle deep analysis involving dynamic runtimes from 30 seconds to 10 minutes — handling retries, failures, and other edge cases.
4. Engineering across the stack to implement feature requests and optimise workflow execution.
5. CI/CD via GitHub Actions, centralised monitoring and maintenance.

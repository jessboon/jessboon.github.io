Tech lead for a financial crime platform for complex graph analysis of people networks using a variety of data sources.

**Role:** Lead Software Engineer · 2025 H1

**Stack:** Python, Celery, AWS, NodeJS, React, Pandas, Redis, PostgreSQL, S3, Bedrock

**Goal:** Design and architect a platform for financial analysts to perform deep, horizontal analysis across banking customers and their networks to identify potential cases of fraud, CSE and hundreds of other exploits.

<p><strong>Impact:</strong> <span class="impact">Reduce analysis time for red flagged customers by 33%.</span></p>

## Responsibilities

1. Lead a team of 3 software engineers across frontend and backend.
2. Architect async workflow patterns to perform complex heuristic driven analysis across a range of exploit vectors leveraging both deterministic and agentic patterns across transactional and profile / historical transacting patterns.
3. Architect data integrations with synapse, databricks, postgreSQL and other services.
4. Model complex metadata driven result set storing to enable dynamic UI/UX, tracing, and observability.

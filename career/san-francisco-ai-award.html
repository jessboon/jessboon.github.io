San Francisco AI award

May 2026 · San Francisco → Stanford

McKinsey ran a Top AI Initiatives award across the firm. I won it for the financial crime graph analysis platform work — the platform that cut red-flagged customer analysis time by 33%.

With the award, a collection of 8 teams received a trip to attend a Stanford Go & Learn program. Our financial crime team ended up receiving first place.

The go and see event included a conference listening to leaders speculate on the direction of AI and implementations across industry. We walked through AI facilities to see research up close.
